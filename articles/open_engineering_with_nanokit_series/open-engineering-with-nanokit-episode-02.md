## Episode 2: Describing the Platform

Nano Kit's whole pitch is that a landing page should go from idea to live in sixty seconds, built by describing the vision in plain language rather than dragging blocks around a canvas. For Open Engineering, that idea already exists in diagram form: a stack running from the Platform down through Ontology, Product Model, Systems of Record, and Runtime Architecture, through the Kernel, and out into four operating systems and their applications. The job of this episode is simply saying that out loud to the builder.

### SIPOC

| Supplier | Input | Process | Output | Customer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ecosystem architect | The architecture overview diagram and its plain-language description | Describe the idea to Nano Kit's AI generation step | A first full homepage draft, hero section included | Site visitors landing on open-engineering.io |
| Nano Kit AI | Natural-language description, chosen style | Generate a complete landing page instantly from the description | A live, editable draft page | Live customization step (Episode 3) |
| Content owner | The four-dimension architecture, DOS/GOS/ROS/SOS naming | Decide the hero illustration should be the architecture diagram itself | A hero section that teaches the ecosystem in seconds | First-time visitors who have not yet read a single page |

### Saying the architecture out loud

The generation step works by having someone describe their idea and get a full landing page instantly, with every change visible in real time as it happens. For Open Engineering, describing the idea means describing the stack itself: Ontology, Product Model, and Systems of Record feeding a Runtime Architecture, sitting on a Kernel, branching into Detective, Game, Runner, and Star operating systems, each carrying its own applications. That description is the actual brief, not marketing copy invented afterward.

### Why the diagram belongs in the hero slot

A hero illustration built from that same architecture diagram does more work than a slogan ever could: it lets a visitor grasp the shape of the whole ecosystem before reading a single paragraph. Rather than writing several pages explaining what Open Engineering is, the diagram does that job at a glance, exactly the kind of instant comprehension a sixty-second landing page generator is built to produce.

### A draft, not a finished page

What comes out of this step is still just a first live draft. The real shaping, tightening the copy, adjusting the layout, deciding where each section leads next, happens in the live customization pass that Episode 3 covers.
